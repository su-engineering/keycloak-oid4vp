# Code of conduct

We want contributors and users to be able to collaborate respectfully, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and considerate in reviews and discussions.
- Critique the work and explain your reasoning.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Give credit and make room for different perspectives.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. These expectations apply to this project's issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [security@su.engineering](mailto:security@su.engineering). Include links or context needed to understand the incident; avoid circulating private information publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban participants when needed to protect the community. Decisions should account for context, severity, and repeated behavior. Reports will be handled privately to the extent practical for investigation and resolution. A participant may request reconsideration through the same private contact.
